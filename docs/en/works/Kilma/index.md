# Kilma
##### 2024

## Starting point

The climate crisis is already here, yet it is often pushed out of sight. With Kilma, I wanted to show how serious the situation is without making a conventional educational game or another dry warning.

## Idea and story

Kilma consists of four mini-games that exaggerate real consequences of climate change. A seagull eats plastic, dolphins move through oil spills and sheep try to escape rising water. What seems funny at first is based on problems that are already part of our present.

## Making the work

I developed the Android app in Unity. A timer runs while you play. Afterwards, the app shows what you could have done for the environment in the same amount of time. The time spent playing becomes part of the idea.

## My contribution

Kilma is an independent project. I created the concept, storytelling, game design, programming and sound. I also developed all the graphics, the logo and every text, and made all the design decisions.
