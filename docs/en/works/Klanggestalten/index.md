# Klanggestalten
##### 2025

## Starting point

The idea for Klanggestalten came to me in a park in Bremen. Listening to the birds, I noticed how much their singing affected me. I love animals, enjoy watching birds and often simply listen to them.

At the same time, not everyone experiences birdsong in the same way. I wanted to translate this feeling into other forms, so that a moment in nature could be visible and tangible as well as audible.

## Idea and story

The app lets you record a bird’s voice and take that moment home. The recording is sent to a lamp, which plays it back and translates it into light and vibration. It becomes a memory of a place and a moment that you can hear, see and feel.

## Making the work

I built the app in Flutter. It connects via Bluetooth to a lamp controlled by an ESP32. Three LED rings separate the sound into high, middle and low frequencies and translate them into moving light. Touching the lamp’s body lets you feel the recording as vibration.

I 3D-printed the lamp’s body and designed its parts to slot together. The logo, app interface and graphics belong to the same visual world.

## My contribution

From the initial idea, logo, design and story to the app, electronics, 3D modelling and finished prototype, I developed and built the entire project myself.
