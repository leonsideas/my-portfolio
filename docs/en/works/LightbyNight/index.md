# Light At Night
##### 2023

## Starting point

Light At Night was created during a course at the University of Bremen. Together with two other students, I developed a tile-based game in which light is part of the gameplay, rather than simply setting the mood.

## Idea and story

The player has to keep a fire burning to survive the night. If it goes out, the game ends. Meanwhile, a wolf roams the world. Players must collect wood, keep moving and avoid straying too far from the protection of the fire.

## Making the work

The game was programmed in Processing. Its story is not explained in words, but emerges through the darkness, fire and decisions made while playing.

## Collaboration

The game was made as a group project with two other students. We developed the concept, mechanics, visual design and implementation together.
