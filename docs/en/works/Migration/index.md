# Migration
##### 2025

## Starting point

In political debates, it often seems to me as though people are simply moved back and forth like game pieces. Yet these are real people with real lives. Migration grew out of this contradiction.

## Idea and story

The table is deliberately childlike, but it is intended for adults. Visitors sit down, move figures along bead-maze wires and experience how easily a complex journey of displacement can be made to look like a simple game.

This contrast is at the centre of the work. Adults, too, need to sit down and ask: what is actually happening here? How do we talk about people, and how easily are political decisions made when the individual lives behind them remain invisible?

Three routes focus on the Sahel region, the Darién Gap and the Mediterranean.

## Making the work

Visitors place 3D-printed figures on marked areas of the table. Light sensors detect their positions and send signals via Arduino to a Processing sketch. A projector then displays relevant information directly on the tabletop. I CNC-milled the lettering and finished it by hand.

## My contribution

I carried out the entire project myself: from the idea, research and design to building the table, sensors, projection, 3D printing and programming.
