# MOI
##### 2025

## Starting point

Laura and I chose the theme together and both read texts by Karen Barad. Our starting point was her idea of intra-action: things do not simply exist on their own, but change through their relationships. From this, I developed the idea and concept for MOI as a way of showing people and nature as interconnected.

## Idea and story

MOI brings together two very different kinds of movement: stones and a dancing body. Stable Diffusion connects these visual worlds, creating images in which bodies, materials and landscapes merge.

## Making the work

I analysed the data from both recordings with a Python programme and linked them together. I then connected this data with Stable Diffusion to let the two visual worlds blend into one another.

The video is accompanied by field recordings and a piano composition written and performed by Laura Eimeranns. There is no conventional plot. The story emerges through repetition, change and the interplay of image and sound.

## Collaboration

MOI was created together with Laura Eimeranns. Laura conceived and recorded the dance performance herself. She also composed the music and performed it on the piano. We brought the recordings together collaboratively.

My contributions were the project idea and concept, the stone recordings, the analysis and linking of both datasets in Python, and their connection to Stable Diffusion.
