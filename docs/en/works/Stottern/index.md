# Stottern
##### 2024

## Starting point

The starting point of the film is personal: I stutter. I know the struggle of wanting to say something while feeling that I have to speak perfectly.

## Idea and story

The film explores this pressure, both in speaking and in the way we relate to ourselves. At its centre is the contradiction of being imperfect while constantly being expected to be perfect.

My own experience forms the emotional core. From it, I created a futuristic world in which every error is corrected. Anyone who does not function smoothly is treated as a malfunction.

## Making the work

I combined live-action footage with animation made in After Effects. Animated glitches make the character’s inner experience visible and heighten the tension between external pressure and the desire to decide for oneself.

## My contribution

I developed the idea, story, visual design and all of the film’s animation.
