# Übergangsobjekte
##### 2026

## Starting point

The project began with Byung-Chul Han’s book *Non-things* and the question of how we relate to objects today. From my own childhood, I remember soft toys and other playthings as objects that provide comfort and hold memories. Today, the smartphone is our constant companion.

## Idea and story

Has the smartphone itself become a transitional object? Or has it already displaced traditional transitional objects? Rather than providing a definitive answer, the work opens up a discussion.

I developed four toys for this purpose. Each character has its own fictional backstory and represents a different aspect of the tension between smartphones and traditional transitional objects.

## Making the work

The visual language deliberately recalls collectible figures and the toys I remember from childhood. A familiar consumer object becomes a way of approaching a much more personal question.

## Exhibition

The transitional objects were shown in *Return of the Gizmos* at Güterbahnhof Bremen and at the University of the Arts Bremen’s open days in 2026.

## My contribution

I developed the research, characters, backstories, visual design, packaging and production of the objects. I also designed the project’s logo.
