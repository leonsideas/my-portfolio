# Mit KI läufts bei uns!
##### 2024

## Starting point

The project began in late 2023 and was my first intensive encounter with a range of AI tools. I was interested not only in the images and videos AI could generate, but also in how it might change the way we tell stories and share knowledge.

## Idea and story

Under the title “Mit KI läufts bei uns!”, I developed a campaign concept for the Übersee-Museum Bremen. It proposes more surprising, accessible and engaging ways of communicating the museum’s content.

A longer film and several short formats connect museum knowledge with the visual language of different AI tools. The project is primarily a concept and an exploration of what might become possible in the future.

## Making the work

I used AI to generate images, videos, music and voices, then selected, edited and connected them to build a shared world. The stories and knowledge from the museum remain at the centre of the work.

## Presentation

The project was shown at the University of the Arts Bremen’s open days in 2024.

## My contribution

I developed and produced everything myself: the campaign idea, texts, visual worlds, trailer, music, voices and reels.
